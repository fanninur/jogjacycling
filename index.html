Why Jogja by Bike

A gentle ride through history, culture, and green lanes.

Yogyakarta is perfect for cycling. The city center is flat, villages hide behind every main road, and friendly locals wave from their doorsteps. Our routes move at an easy pace so you can absorb the sounds, sights, and small surprises of Javanese life.
